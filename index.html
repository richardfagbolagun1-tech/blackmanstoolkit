This is for you brother.

Welcome. Take a breath. You have opened something that was built with you in mind.

This toolkit is a gift from Lambeth, South London to Black men everywhere. It is the work of Black Thrive Lambeth, a partnership of community organisations, NHS, and council, designed in 2025 with The Office of Art and Technology: a design studio that builds tools with communities, not just for them.

This toolkit will not lecture you. It will not pretend racism does not exist or that one walk a day fixes everything. What it will do is give you six honest conversations: about your body, your mind, your money, your spirit, your brothers, and your community.

If you are a queer or trans Black man, this toolkit is for you too. You will find resources written with you in mind, not as an afterthought.

Use this at your pace. Skip around. Read one section, then close the page and come back next week. Share it with a brother, a cousin, a son. There is no right way to use this, except honestly.
