Challenges

Eighteen honest, doable things, lifted straight from the chapters. Tick one off when you have actually done it. You are the only judge here, so be honest. That is the whole point.

Low on energy today? You do not need a single one of these, and you do not need a streak. Open the Feelings Wheel or the De-Mask instead, and come back when you have more in the tank.

The reward is the thing you did. Nothing else.

We are not going to dangle vouchers or points you can cash in. The men we spoke with were clear that effort already goes unrecognised in their lives. So this is the recognition: a record, kept by you and for you, of every real step you took.

Read a chapter, you earn steps. Check in, you earn steps. Do one of the eighteen things, you earn steps. Refreshing the page earns nothing. The number only moves when you do.
