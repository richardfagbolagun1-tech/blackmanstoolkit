Find a therapist

Find a therapist who looks like you.

Brothers told us again and again: a therapist who understands the racism conversation without you having to explain it is the difference between therapy that works and therapy that wastes your money.

BAATN, the Black, African and Asian Therapy Network, has over 1,300 qualified therapists of Black, African, Asian, and Caribbean heritage across the UK. Filter by location, fee, specialism, language, gender. Many also signal LGBTQ-affirming practice.

Search BAATN's directory
Three tips before you book
  1. Most therapists offer a free 15 minute call before the first session. Use it. You are interviewing them too.
  2. If the first one is not right, it is not the end. Try another. This is normal.
  3. NHS Talking Therapies is free at the point of use. Ask your GP for an IAPT referral, or search NHS Talking Therapies plus your borough.
