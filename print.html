For the wallet, the counter, the mirror

Take this off the screen.

The toolkit is also paper. Print the pocket cards, slip them in your wallet, drop the chapter PDFs on the counter at your barbers, give them to your son when he turns 16.

1 · The Pocket Pack

Five cards. One wallet.

Each card is credit-card sized. Print double-sided, cut along the dotted line, slip them in your wallet. The scripts go where you need them, not where the wifi is.

Card 1

The Field Guide.

Six scripts for when your doctor is not listening.

 Card 2

The Feelings Wheel.

When fine or stressed is not the truth. Print, fold, keep.

 Card 3

The Vetting Card.

Pub friend on one side. Vault on the other. Write in by hand.

 Card 4

The De-Mask.

Five minutes to take the armour off before you walk in.

 Card 5

The Value Sheet.

Net worth is not self worth. Three questions, one card.
